Conditional Forecasts and Risk Scenarios

A MATLAB toolbox for conditional forecasting in Bayesian VARs Replication and extension, 2026
Description

A self-contained MATLAB package that replicates and extends the risk-scenario exercise of Moran, Stevanovic and Surprenant (2025), Bank of Canada Staff Working Paper 2025–28, Risk Scenarios and Macroeconomic Forecasts. It estimates a monthly Bayesian VAR under a Minnesota prior, produces a baseline forecast, and imposes alternative paths for selected variables using the Waggoner–Zha conditional forecasting algorithm, with the Baumeister–Kilian approach available as a robustness check. Four scenarios are implemented out of the box — an oil price spike, a US recession, a tight labour market, and an additional round of monetary tightening — alongside forecast error variance decompositions and structural impulse responses. Canadian data come from Statistics Canada and FRED. The code runs without any MATLAB toolbox and is compatible with Octave. [Edit this description as you like.]
