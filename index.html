Job Market Paper

High-Frequency Surprises and Low-Frequency Identification Draft available upon request
Abstract

High-frequency monetary policy surprises are widely used as external instruments in structural VARs, but the same surprise can be a clean instrument for some blocks of the system and a contaminated one for others. I develop a hybrid proxy-SVAR that allows the contamination of an instrument to differ across variable blocks, characterize the resulting bias, and propose an estimator that assigns instruments to blocks rather than imposing a single validity condition on the whole system.
